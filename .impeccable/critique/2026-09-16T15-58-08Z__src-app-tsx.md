## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** The artifact is authored and un-copyable. The cassette lifts on an arc, turns flat, enters the deck as the flap closes and the status window flips STANDBY → LOADING → ▶ PLAY, while the page chrome dissolves and the studio grows out of its own box; eject retraces the path with the deck reading EJECT. The reader inside the tube (OSD, uppercase Archivo 800 title, VT323 metadata, bracketed tags, REC line) is a second typographic world that exists only because the CRT exists. Print discipline (one label per key cap, status window, AV–01 line, spine number and name, idle screen) and the matte-graphite-with-one-blue-light palette hold the "modern-clean, never aged" brief on every frame inspected: no grunge, no sepia, scanlines and grain only inside the tube.

The shell around it is where the site could be anyone's: nameplate plus two nav links, a tracked-caps kicker ("INDEPENDENT MIND. HANDS-ON MAKER."), a two-line display with the second line dimmed, a short right-aligned aside, a hairline card grid, a slogan footer ("Made with intention. A little nostalgia, too."). This is the current default of the dark design-engineer portfolio, executed carefully (the One Column Rule is real; the mark hangs in the gutter) but with category-interchangeable copy. Only "one tape at a time" ties the prose to the object. The index cards are the exception: number-over-name with a 2 px accent strip reads as a spine, and the dashed "Coming soon… / Blank tape" cells are an honest, specific way to hold empty slots.

Missed character: playback removes every trace of identity (header inert at opacity 0), so a visitor arriving mid-tape sees a machine with no name on it; the shell's type never borrows the equipment's language beyond tracked caps; and on phones the thesis, the insertion, never plays. Tap opens a native sheet, so the most-shared device gets an isometric picture and a modal.

**Deterministic scan.** CLI: 1 finding in `src` and `index.html`, `border-accent-on-rounded` at `src/components/DeckControls.module.css:18`. It is the documented keycap "darker bottom edge" (DESIGN.md, Playback buttons): false positive. Every detector hit is a documented exception or a library artifact; the detector confirms the design system is being followed rather than finding drift. What it caught that the review missed: the 404 screen's class attribute is `_screen_… undefined` because `CRT.tsx:36` composes `styles[mode]` and the module defines `.idle`, `.ejecting` and `.playing` but no `.nosignal`; the header identity link is 39.9 px tall and the footer GitHub/LinkedIn links 42 px tall, under the 44 px floor the rest of the site keeps; every scene load logs `THREE.Clock: This module has been deprecated`. Where both agree: 11 px is the smallest text anywhere (kicker, index numbers, edition, AV–01 mark), all at 5.63:1 or better; no horizontal overflow at any viewport; zero console errors, including the no-WebGL context.

## Overall Impression

The studio is the portfolio piece the brief asks for, and the insertion is the claim a neighbouring portfolio could not truthfully copy. The work now sits in the seams: the peak moment carries a wrong instruction, the phone is told to pick from targets it cannot hit, keyboard focus is parked offscreen after the exit, common laptops drop the reader under its own 16 px floor, and a shared link previews as nothing. The single biggest opportunity is the phone: the device most shared links open on never sees the mechanism and cannot reliably select in the studio.

## What's Working

1. **The mechanism is a machine, not a transition.** The cassette follows a physical path, the flap and the status window belong to the same timeline, the canvas keeps drawing the studio where the page box had it while the frame grows, and eject retraces the path beneath a pointer-transparent layer. Verified frame by frame at 0.3, 0.8, 1.4, 2.0 and 2.6 s in, and 0.4, 1.1 and 1.8 s out.
2. **Print and light discipline.** Only the CRT emits; the deck's one colour print is the muted-sound slash; the idle studio blue is the single saturated field and the cassette accents recur as 2 px strips in the index. The equipment reads as modeled objects (24-sided dial, vents, pads, parting lines) with no pixelation.
3. **Accessibility is structural, not bolted on.** Tapes are real links with a visible 2 px cyan ring and arrow-key movement; playback is a labelled dialog with the shell inert, a focus loop that includes the named scrollable article, and a polite live region; reduced motion swaps in 120 ms; deep links open a readable native reader instantly and hand over in one dissolve; no-WebGL keeps the full index and reader with zero console output.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Phones are told to select in the studio, where the tapes are 15–27 px wide.** At 390×844 the 3D tapes answer the pointer in columns of 18 px (ALPHA), 15 px (BETA), 18 px (GAMMA) and 27 px (ABOUT), measured by sweep, while the guide reads "Select a cassette in the studio." On touch there is no hover-confirm step, so a mis-tap commits to a full-screen reader and costs an eject. **Why it matters:** the visitor on the most-shared device cannot reliably do the one thing the page asks. **Fix:** on narrow or touch canvases give the invisible slot targets a 44 px screen-space minimum (scale `SLOT_TARGET` per slot from the camera zoom, or resolve the nearest slot within a 44 px radius on a canvas tap), or re-aim the guide on phones at the index ("Pick a tape from the index below") and treat the studio as a picture there. Files: `src/components/studio/transport.ts`, `src/components/studio/Tape.tsx`, `src/components/studio/BlankTape.tsx`, `src/components/Stage.tsx` (guide copy), `src/components/studio/framing.ts`. **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt
2. **[P2] The peak moment carries a contradictory instruction.** During insertion the idle texture prints "LOADING TAPE" and, beneath it, "CHOOSE A TAPE / TO PLAY" (`StudioScene.tsx:357–367`: the sub-line branches on `preview` only, never on `inserting`). **Why it matters:** these are the most-watched 2.4 s of the site, and the copy denies what the visitor just did. **Fix:** branch the sub-lines on `inserting` (blank, or the tape's name, or "PLEASE WAIT"), and consider printing the tape name in the LOADING state. **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish
3. **[P2] The exit loses keyboard users.** At 1440×900 Escape returns focus to the tape link at y=932 in a 900 px viewport with `preventScroll`, so nothing visibly changes; escaping `/nothing-here` leaves focus on `<body>` (`lastTape` is null for a 404); during insertion focus sits on `<body>` and Skip is never focused, so any key skips but nothing says so; the modeled 404 offers sighted visitors no visible way back beyond recognising the EJECT cap. **Why it matters:** a screen-reader or keyboard user does not know where they are after the exit; one more Tab happens to work, which is a workaround, not a design. **Fix:** after the return, `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })` on the restored link or focus `#archive-title` when the index is out of view; on a 404 exit focus the archive heading or the identity link; focus Skip during insertion; print an "EJECT / ESC" line on the NO SIGNAL screen. File: `src/components/Stage.tsx`. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden
4. **[P2] Common laptops break the reader's 16 px floor, and nothing says the article scrolls.** At 1280×720 the tube scale is 0.90, so prose renders at 15.3 px; at 1366×768 it is 16.4 px. The full-height reader only takes over at heights up to 699 px. The article also ends mid-sentence at the tube's bottom edge with no fade, and the scrollbar is seam-lit on black at 1.8:1. **Why it matters:** the reading experience is the payoff, and the visitor cannot tell the write-up continues. **Fix:** raise the height breakpoint to about 780 px or derive the Html `distanceFactor` so prose never drops under 16 px; mirror the OSD's top gradient at the bottom of the reader and pick a scrollbar colour at 3:1 or better. Files: `src/components/Stage.tsx` (the `(max-width: 767px), (max-height: 699px)` query), `src/components/CRT.module.css`, `src/components/studio/StudioScene.tsx`. **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt
5. **[P2] Shared links preview as nothing, and a deep link opens on an unnamed machine.** Every route, 404 included, has the title "Daniel Alyoshin — Design Engineer"; `index.html` has no `og:` or `twitter:` meta; a desktop deep link's first second shows a black sheet whose only identity is an 11 px "AV–01 / VHS" label, then dissolves into a studio with no name on it. **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md makes sharing the success metric; a peer pasting `/project/placeholder-beta` into Slack gets a generic title and no image, and a visitor arriving mid-tape does not learn whose site it is. **Fix:** set the title per tape ("Placeholder: Beta — Daniel Alyoshin", "No signal — Daniel Alyoshin"); add OG and Twitter tags with a rendered studio still; let one line of identity survive the dissolve (the nameplate, or the name printed in the OSD). Files: `index.html`, `src/components/Stage.tsx`. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden
6. **[P3] Naming drift and small craft debts.** "The archive" / "The tape index" / "Skip to tape archive" / "ALYOSHIN ARCHIVE" for one thing; four labels for the About tape; the guide prints the full spine label while the index prints its first segment; the fallback keeps "Select a cassette in the studio." with no studio; the native eject key's accessible name is "Eject tape ESC" because the `<kbd>` joins it; the 404 screen's `undefined` class; the identity link at 39.9 px and footer links at 42 px; the `THREE.Clock` deprecation warning peers will see in devtools. ## Minor Observations

- The title's 3 px focus underline shows on direct load and canvas clicks but not on index-link clicks; on the 390 px reader it breaks across "PLACEHOLDER: / ALPHA" and reads heavy.
- The modeled EJECT cap's hover lightening (`#4d5865` → `#66727e`) is real but subtle enough to miss; the status-window sound mark changes correctly.
- In browse at 1440×900 the deck prints render at 4–6 px cap height, fine as decoration, illegible as information; at 1280×720 the whole studio is 370 px tall and the spine names are unreadable.
- Beta's three-line title takes 25% of the tube; set a long title one step smaller. Gamma (one paragraph, one tag) fits without scrolling and looks intentional.
- The About reader ends "If you want to talk shop, the links are below." with the OSD links inside the tube: the one place the visit has an ending. The browse page's footer does not.
- History Back ejects and Forward re-seats the tape and reopens the modeled reader with focus on the title. Page scroll survives a playback cycle. Both correct.
- The mobile native reader stacks three rounded surfaces (screen, bezel, fixed reader with a 20 px page-ground spread). It reads as a device, but it is the one place the shell has nested cards.
- The `.tapeComing` dashed border at 1.8:1 is fine as a non-text cue; "Coming soon…" is 5.63:1.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the insertion is the thesis, why does the phone never see it? Would a shorter, front-on insertion into the full-height reader be worth more than a perfect one that is desktop-only?
2. Two selection surfaces for six items: which is primary? If the studio, the index is a legend and should look like one; if the index (keyboard, mobile, no-WebGL), the guide should point there on those devices instead of at 18 px spines.
3. Playback removes every trace of whose site this is. Is "AV–01" enough identity for someone who arrives mid-tape, or does one line of the shell need to survive the dissolve?
4. How much VCR trivia is flavour and how much is noise to a design engineer born after VHS? Would a runtime that means something, like reading time, make the OSD honest rather than ornamental?
5. Is the end of the visit designed? After the last eject the page runs out at two footer links. Where is the final beat that turns recognition into reaching out?
